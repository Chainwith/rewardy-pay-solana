## Solana integration

Solana is Rewardy Pay's settlement and verification layer. A customer signs the
payment from a wallet. The merchant settlement and Rewardy fee are executed
atomically, while the payment reference connects the on-chain transaction to the
off-chain order. Rewardy marks a payment paid and enables its reward only after
independently verifying the signer, mint, recipients, amounts and finality.
